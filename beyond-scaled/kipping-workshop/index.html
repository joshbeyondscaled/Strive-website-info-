The method

The PPP Framework

You can't kip what you can't hold.

  1. Position

    Hollow, arch and an active hang. The most important step and the one most people get wrong. Without it, nothing else lands.

  2. Pattern

    Link the positions in time and space with toe-spotted box drills, so your body knows where to be under the bar and at the top.

  3. Power

    Hip pop, not leg raise. Take the box away and turn the swing into a pull-up.

Position → Pattern → Power. Always in that order.
