2. What I collect

Information you give me

  • Coaching information: training history, goals, availability, equipment, body measurements, and answers to questionnaires and check-ins.
  • Health information: injuries, medical conditions, medication, pain, mental wellbeing, nutrition and lifestyle information, and health questionnaires (for example a PAR-Q). This is "special category" data and gets extra protection (see section 4).
  • Photos and videos: training clips and progress photos you send me or post in the community.
  • Messages: anything you send me by email, SMS, WhatsApp, DM, in an app or in the community.
  • Payment details: billing name, address and payment history. Card and bank details go straight to Stripe or GoCardless. I never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Website and advertising data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, and which ad or link brought you here (for example UTM tags, fbclid). Cookies and pixels are only used with your consent (see section 9).
  • Email and SMS data: whether messages are delivered, opened or clicked.

4. Your health information

Health information is only collected when you choose to give it, and only used to coach you safely. I ask for your explicit consent when you first share it, for example on a health questionnaire or onboarding form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing me. If you do, I may not be able to keep coaching you safely, and I'll tell you if that's the case.

Health information is not used for advertising, and it is never shared with anyone except the apps I use to deliver your coaching (section 5). If I think you need medical help I'll tell you, but I won't contact anyone on your behalf unless there's an emergency and a risk to your life.
