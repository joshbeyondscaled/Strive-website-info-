Cookie policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or entries a website stores in your browser. This page explains which ones this site uses.

What we use

  • Strictly necessary storage. When you open the contact form, the site stores an anonymous visitor token in your browser's local storage so the form can load and send your enquiry securely through Wix. It contains no personal details.
  • Hosting and security. Our hosting provider, Wix, may set cookies needed to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Because these are strictly necessary for the site to work, they don't need your consent under UK law.

What we don't use

No advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking and no analytics. Fonts are served from our own site, not from a third party.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If you do, the contact form may not work. You can always email info@strivecorpwellbeing.co.uk instead.
