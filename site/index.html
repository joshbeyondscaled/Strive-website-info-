The problem

Wellbeing that changes something.

A one-off talk, a fruit bowl or an app nobody opens won't shift sickness absence, energy at work or how your teams talk to each other. People need simple habits, real coaching and someone checking in.

That's what we build. We take responsibility for keeping your people fit, healthy and active, and we bring in specialists where they add value. Wellbeing isn't a four-week project, so we don't treat it like one.

Investing in wellbeing is the ultimate catalyst for sustainable business performance.

Why it matters

Ill health is one of the biggest costs most businesses never measure.

Lost to sickness, 2025 148.8m

Working days lost to sickness or injury across the UK, an average of 4.4 days per worker.

Office for National Statistics, May 2026
Absence per employee 9.4 days

The average a year, the highest in over 15 years and up from 5.8 days in 2022.

CIPD and Simplyhealth, September 2025
Stress, depression, anxiety 22.1m

Working days lost to work-related cases in 2024/25, with each case lasting 22.9 days on average.

Health and Safety Executive, November 2025
Cost to employers £85bn

A year to UK employers through sickness, staff turnover and lost productivity.

Department for Work and Pensions and Department for Business and Trade, November 2025
The return £4.70

Average return for every £1 employers invest in supporting their employees' mental health.

How it works

From first call to clear results.

  1. Discovery call

    20 minutes on your people, goals and budget. You get a free quote on the call.

  2. Baseline

    A short survey and, where it helps, health monitoring bands that track sleep, heart rate and activity.

  3. Programme

    Challenges, coaching and workshops, on site or online, often starting with a pilot cohort.

  4. Results

    A clear report for leadership, and a plan to roll out to the rest of your teams.
