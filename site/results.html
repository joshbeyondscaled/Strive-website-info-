Eric Wright Group · Walton Summit

The February Reset: a 6-week wellbeing challenge

The challenge
Give employees a practical, structured way to build healthier habits at the start of the year, open to everyone whatever their starting point.
What we did
Six weekly live seminars, each ending with one habit to track in the app: a daily walk, 2 litres of water, protein in the first meal, no caffeine after 2pm, a short daily reflection and 10 minutes of deliberate exercise. Employees also had on-demand workouts, nutrition resources and daily access to a coach through PT in your pocket.
What landed
Nutrition was the topic employees engaged with most. Participants who completed the end-of-challenge survey reported feeling healthier, and the challenge has shaped how we run every programme since.
Eric Wright Water

Lifestyle and nutrition seminars

What we did
An introductory seminar for staff, followed by lifestyle and nutrition presentations.
Why it matters
Seminars build trust and buy-in first, so people feel confident getting involved in a longer programme.
How we measure

Every programme ends with a clear report.

You see what changed, for how many people and over what time. Individual data stays private: leadership only ever sees anonymised, grouped results.

  1. Baseline survey

    A short survey at the start on energy, sleep, stress, activity and time off sick.

  2. Health monitoring

    Optional bands that track heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and activity, day to day.

  3. Habit tracking

    Weekly habits logged in the app, so we can see what people actually stick with.

  4. Report for leadership

    The same survey at the end, compared with the baseline, plus what we recommend next.
