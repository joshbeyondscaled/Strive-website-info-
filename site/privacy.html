Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Strive Wellbeing ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you visit this website, send us an enquiry, or take part in one of our programmes. We are the data controller for this information.

Contact: info@strivecorpwellbeing.co.uk, 07793 458864.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, work email, phone number (optional), company, team size, the programmes you're interested in and your message.
  • Clients and programme participants: contact details, attendance, survey answers and, only where you agree to wear one, data from a health monitoring band (heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and activity).
  • Website use: basic technical data our hosting provider needs to run the site securely, such as your IP address and browser type.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps before entering a contract.
  • To deliver a programme your employer has agreed with us: performance of that contract and our legitimate interests.
  • Health data from bands, surveys or coaching: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Results we share with your employer are anonymised and grouped, never individual.
  • To keep records for tax and accounting: legal obligation.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Wix.com, which hosts this website and stores enquiry form submissions on our behalf.
  • The provider of our coaching app and health monitoring bands, for participants who use them.
  • Specialist practitioners we bring in to deliver part of a programme (for example physiotherapy or counselling), only the details they need.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: 24 months from your last contact, then deleted.
  • Client contracts and invoices: the length of the contract plus 6 years, for tax purposes.
  • Programme participant data: for the programme plus 12 months, then deleted or fully anonymised.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, take a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email info@strivecorpwellbeing.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the date at the top.
