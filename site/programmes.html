Structured challenges

Structured Wellbeing Challenges

Short, high-impact programmes that build team momentum through simple habits.

Who it's for
Any company with more than 10 employees
Format
On site, online or hybrid
Length
4 to 8 weeks
Pricing
Tailored. Free quote on your discovery call

What's included

  • A structured challenge built around sleep, food, movement and connection
  • Team leaderboards and tracking through our online coaching app, so challenges can run fully remotely
  • In-person sessions and check-ins where you want them
  • Optional health monitoring bands to measure real change
  • A results summary for leadership at the end
Coaching-led

Coaching-Led Programmes

Ongoing support with structured plans, regular check-ins and accountability.

Who it's for
Teams of 10 or more at a time
Larger sites
Run in cohorts, starting with a pilot
Length
Ongoing
Pricing
Tailored. Free quote on your discovery call

Support we can include

  • Personal training and coaching
  • Massage therapy
  • Physiotherapy and occupational health
  • Counselling
  • Yoga and Pilates sessions
  • Wellbeing initiatives tailored to your teams

Workshops

Workshops & Education

Practical sessions that give teams tools they can use day to day.

Length
45 to 60 minutes
Format
In person (best received) or remote
Group size
No limit for talks; interactive sessions sized to you
Pricing
Typically three workshops for £900

Topics we cover

  • Motivation and mindset
  • Nutrition
  • Training and exercise
  • Flexibility and mobility
  • Workplace posture
  • Diabetes
  • Weight management
  • Anything your workplace needs to talk about: we bring in specialists where needed

Prices vary with the topic, travel, group size and what's needed to deliver. We fit sessions around lunch breaks or working hours.

PT in your pocket

Who takes part

Length

Pricing
